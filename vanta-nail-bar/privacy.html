Privacy Policy

How Vanta Nail Bar handles information shared through our website and booking channels.

Vanta Nail Bar (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This page explains what information we may collect when you contact us, book an appointment, or interact with our website.

Information We Collect

When you reach out or book, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, appointment preferences, and message details. If you opt in, we may also process SMS marketing or transactional messaging preferences.

How We Use Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, schedule and confirm appointments, provide customer service, and — only with consent — send promotional or transactional texts. Consent to marketing texts is not required to purchase services.

SMS Preferences

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from marketing texts and HELP for assistance.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate booking, communications, or website tools, solely as needed to provide those services.
